About

I am an M.S. student at POSTECH, advised by Namhoon Lee. I am broadly interested in efficient neural networks — making models smaller and faster to run without giving up accuracy.

My work centers on model compression, including pruning and quantization, with a focus on compressing large models while preserving their downstream performance.
