Reusing the KV cache

2026.08.20 · Hyeondo Jang

The invariant that makes reuse legal

Decode computes one row. Suppose $t$ tokens have been processed and the model needs the output at position $t+1$. Inside one head of one layer, attention is defined on the whole sequence, but only its last row is ever used:

\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} \vect{o}_{t+1} &= \left[\softmax\!\left(\frac{\mat{Q}\mat{K}\T}{\sqrt{\dhead}} + \mat{M}\right)\mat{V} \right]_{t+1,\,:} \\[3pt] &= \left[\softmax\!\left(\frac{\mat{Q}\mat{K}\T}{\sqrt{\dhead}} + \mat{M}\right) \right]_{t+1,\,:}\mat{V} \\[3pt] &= \softmax\!\left(\frac{\mat{Q}_{t+1,\,:}\,\mat{K}\T}{\sqrt{\dhead}}\right)\mat{V} \\[3pt] &= \softmax\!\left(\frac{\vect{q}\,\mat{K}\T}{\sqrt{\dhead}}\right)\mat{V} \end{aligned} \end{equation}

Two facts do the work. Row $t+1$ of a product is row $t+1$ of the left factor times the right factor, and $\softmax$ acts row by row, so the row index passes through both. The causal mask $\mat{M}$ is vacuous on the last row — position $t+1$ may attend to everything before it — so it drops out. What remains is a single query row $\vect{q} = \mat{Q}_{t+1,\,:} \in \R^{1\times\dhead}$ against $\mat{K},\mat{V} \in \R^{(t+1)\times\dhead}$: the full $\mat{Q}$ never has to be formed, but $\mat{K}$ and $\mat{V}$ are needed in full. That is what the cache holds.

And $\mat{K}$, $\mat{V}$ are only ever appended to. Row $i$ of the layer-$\ell$ cache is

\begin{equation} \vect{k}_i = \Rope_i\!\big(\vect{x}^{(\ell-1)}_i\mat{W}_K\big), \qquad \vect{v}_i = \vect{x}^{(\ell-1)}_i\mat{W}_V \end{equation}

and $\vect{x}^{(\ell-1)}_i$ is a function of $t_1,\dots,t_i$ alone: causal attention lets no later position reach it, and every other stage in the block is row-wise. So the step that produces position $t+1$ writes one new row and leaves the others untouched,

\begin{equation} \mat{K}_{1:t+1} = \begin{bmatrix} \mat{K}_{1:t} \\ \vect{k}_{t+1} \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mat{V}_{1:t+1} = \begin{bmatrix} \mat{V}_{1:t} \\ \vect{v}_{t+1} \end{bmatrix} \end{equation}

Whether row $i$ was written during prefill or during an earlier decode step, it is the same row, and it is the row every later step reads. Nothing recomputes it to a different value. Stated for the cache as a whole, with $\KVc_{1:S}$ the first $S$ entries over all layers and heads:

\begin{equation} \KVc_{1:S}\big(t_{1:T}\big) \;=\; \KVc_{1:S}\big(t_{1:S}\big) \qquad\text{for every } S \le T \end{equation}

The cache is prefix-closed: what follows a prefix never changes the prefix's entries. So they stay valid for the rest of this sequence, and for any other sequence starting the same way.
